Privacy Policy - Rose Harlow Puzzles Pin Scheduler

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Rose Harlow Puzzles Pin Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owner of the Rose Harlow Puzzles Pinterest account. It is not offered to other people and has no other users.

What it does

The tool connects to the owner's own Pinterest account through the Pinterest API to (1) read the owner's own list of boards, so Pins are saved to the right board, and (2) create Pins of the owner's own books, with a title, description, image and a link to the book on Amazon.

What data it accesses

Only the owner's own Pinterest boards and Pins. It does not access, collect, store or share any data about other Pinterest users.

Storage and security

The Pinterest access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared with anyone. The tool keeps a local log of which Pins it has published (Pin title and date). No personal data is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in Pinterest settings (Security > Apps), which immediately stops all access.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the owner through the Rose Harlow Puzzles Pinterest profile.
